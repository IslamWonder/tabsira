## 4 October 2026 — start of the rewrite

1. **Scope.** v2, plus the atlas and camera extension, plus a core social network. There are no day-by-day cuts: everything is built; unfinished features stay behind their feature flag and never break the core journey.
2. **Social network («تبصرة تواصل»)** replaces v2 §18's "public page only": posts made from verified Basiras, a feed with «لك» and «أتابع» tabs and cursor pagination, follow, like, comments, bookmark, report, block, and moderation states (`draft`, `pending_review`, `published`, `rejected`, `removed`) with an AI guard. Out of scope: reposts, polls, mentions, push notifications, direct messages.
3. **Lens is dropped**, including the extension's «انظر بعين هذه البصيرة». The hidden treasure (v2 §17) is the only "return" mechanic.
4. **AI providers.** OVH (Qwen) and OpenAI are both wired behind one adapter, one settings block per provider. The benchmark runs first; its measured result sets the default model of each stage.
5. **Detector.** Ultralytics YOLOE / YOLO-World in `services/vision`, reached only over HTTP and behind the `DETECTOR` interface so it can be swapped. Ultralytics is AGPL-3.0, so `services/vision` is AGPL-3.0. The licence of the rest of the repository is **pending the owners' choice**; no `LICENSE` file is added until then.
6. **Corpora.**
   - Quran: `final_complete_verses_20251202_194512.json` (Firas's annotated corpus). Its text is checked at import against the Tanzil Uthmani (Hafs) edition and the result is recorded in `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.
   - Hadith displayed text: the nine books from the fawazahmed0 `hadith-api` Arabic editions, with grades exactly as given.
   - `processed_sunnah_data.json`: ranking signals only, after a lossless cp720 repair. Never displayed.
7. **Design gate.** Three directions are published for the owners. No interface work starts before `docs/DESIGN_DECISION.md` records a choice; API, data, CI and deployment proceed meanwhile.
8. **Photos.** Stored with the account owner's consent in S3-compatible storage in production and on local disk on `tabsira.test`, under v2 §19's rules. A photo is copied to a public location only when its owner publishes.
9. **Atlas and camera, adapted as follows.**
   - Place search and reverse lookup use GeoNames imported into the `geodata` schema, so no geocoding request leaves our servers.
   - The map uses MapLibre with OpenFreeMap tiles. These tiles are the one documented exception to "no third-party request from the visitor's browser", named in the terms page.
   - The camera discovery ships levels A (places) and B (direction); level C (anchoring) stays behind a flag and is not announced until it is proven on devices.
10. **Domains.** Production `tabsira.me`; local development `tabsira.test` with mkcert certificates.
11. **Delivery tooling:** Jenkinsfile with a SonarQube scan and quality gate, ruff for Python, Biome for TypeScript and JSON, prettier for Markdown, a check-only pre-commit hook, the `app` and `geodata` schemas with two Alembic chains, and zero-downtime deploys (pm2 reload one instance at a time; gunicorn workers replaced one at a time). Jenkins and SonarQube addresses are configuration, never hard-coded.
12. **Git.** One logical change per commit, a one-line message, no co-author or tool attribution. Agents commit locally; the owners push.
13. **TimescaleDB** for append-only time series, as hypertables partitioned on their time column: scan stage events and timings, AI calls (provider, model, tokens, cost, latency), evidence and insight exposures, feed and map impressions, moderation actions and the admin audit log. Ordinary tables keep ordinary timestamps; retention and compression policies are set per hypertable.
14. **Admin area:** sqladmin at `/admin` on the API, sign-in with a real account that has `is_admin`, two-factor code when enrolled, twelve-hour session, CSRF, rate limit and an audit log. Views: users, profiles, insights, posts, comments, reports, blocks, moderation queue, map entries and location reports, ontology candidates, learning-path versions, AI cost and latency, GeoNames. **Quran and hadith records are read-only in the admin**: no edit, create or delete form exists for them.
15. **PostgreSQL extensions.** Nine are required: `postgis`, `pg_trgm`, `unaccent`, `pgcrypto`, `btree_gin`, `btree_gist`, `pg_stat_statements`, `vector` (pgvector) and `timescaledb`. They are installed by the provisioning scripts on every host (development, CI image, production) and created by the first migration; a migration never silently skips a missing one. TimescaleDB is a hard requirement, not an optional extra, because it backs real time series (decision 13).
16. **Quran text (supersedes the Quran part of decision 6).** The displayed text is quranpedia.net's «مصحف حفص نسخة نصية» (mushaf id 2, Uthmani script in the King Fahd Complex encoding), imported from quranpedia's official versioned dump with its SHA-256 checked, and rendered with the KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs v22 font that quranpedia serves, self-hosted. Each verse stores its quranpedia id, the dump version and its own hash. A daily job reads quranpedia's changes feed and applies every corrected verse automatically: the new text is stored with its version and hash, the previous one is kept in history, and an audit entry is written. Each verse links to its quranpedia page. Firas's annotated corpus is used for retrieval only, joined by surah and verse number; its text is never displayed.
17. **Hadith corpus (supersedes the hadith part of decision 6).** All nine books: Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah and Malik from fawazahmed0 `hadith-api` (Unlicense); Musnad Ahmad and Sunan al-Darimi from Open-Hadith-Data (ODbL 1.0, credited on the sources page). Grades inside these datasets are informational and never decide eligibility.
18. **Hadith grades come from dorar.net only, recorded by an editor.** dorar.net blocks automated access (Cloudflare, since May 2026) and is never called from the server. An editor opens dorar in a browser and records, in the admin area, the ruling text exactly as dorar gives it, the scholar, the source book and page, the dorar page URL, the date and the editor. The editor also classifies the ruling as صحيح, حسن, ضعيف, موضوع or مختلف فيه; only صحيح and حسن make a hadith eligible as evidence. A hadith the pipeline wanted but that has no ruling yet enters a verification queue ordered by demand; until it is verified, the insight shows the verse alone. Every displayed hadith carries a «تحقق في الدرر» link the reader opens. This replaces the 4 October answers "Bukhari and Muslim plus unanimous grades" and "Arabic glossary of English grades".
19. **HadeethEnc** (hadeethenc.com) is not used, neither for grades nor for explanations.
20. **Delivery order and Docker.** Features come first; production deployment is built once everything works. Production runs on the host without Docker (systemd, gunicorn, pm2, nginx). Docker is not used for deployment; the only container is the throw-away database of a CI build.
21. **Redis** is installed on every host and is used for what PostgreSQL does poorly: publishing scan progress to whichever worker holds the reader's server-sent-events stream, the background job queue that runs scans, short-lived caches (place search, feeds) and rate-limit counters. Durable state stays in PostgreSQL (sessions included).
22. **Production data hosts behind a VPN.** In production, PostgreSQL (with the nine extensions) and Redis run on their own machine, reachable only over the VPN (`deploy/provision-postgres.sh` and `deploy/provision-redis.sh`): listen on loopback and the VPN interface address only (Netbird `wt0` by default, configurable), refuse to bind every interface, `pg_hba` allows the application role from the VPN subnet with scram-sha-256 only, Redis requires a password and protected mode, nightly local dumps. Written with the deployment, after the features (decision 20).
23. **No PostHog** and no other product analytics.
24. **GlitchTip error tracking is optional.** It is enabled only when `GLITCHTIP_DSN` (API) is set; empty means off with no code path sending anything. Mechanism: the Sentry SDK on the API with scrubbing (no photos, no profile fields, no scripture-unrelated personal data, no tokens), and browser errors posted to the API's own `/client-errors` endpoint, which forwards them, so no key ships in the bundle and the visitor's browser makes no third-party request.
25. **Sign-in by email and by Google.** Email accounts verify their address by a mailed link and can reset their password by mail (Arabic transactional mail over SMTP); nothing is sent while SMTP is not configured. An unverified account can sign in and use everything private; a verified email is required before anything is published.
26. **The interface is a AAA, Final Fantasy–grade gamified experience** (see `docs/DESIGN_DECISION.md`, «Game feel»), visibly better than the earlier prototype, whose look is the starting point. The light theme follows the earlier prototype (white paper, gold light, emerald ink); the dark theme is the same craft at night.
27. **Gamification is in scope**, carried over from the earlier prototype's `team/GAMIFICATION.md` under its §0 rules (practice never piety, rules never a model, no new claim about scripture, quiet by default, never leaderboards, comparisons, loss-aversion or reward claims): practice ranks, streak, daily quest «بصيرة اليوم», the sky of meanings, practice badges with locks, the victory banner and burst on «تمّ», and the world's growth animation. Out of scope for now: push reminders and any product analytics (decision 23). `docs/spec/tajriba.md` and `docs/spec/masar.md` are mandatory reading for every interface and learning task, in full.
28. **Google Analytics (GA4) is the one analytics tool** and amends decision 23 ("no product analytics") and the "no third-party request from the visitor's browser" rule for this single case. Configuration is `GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` (empty means GA is off and no Google script is ever loaded), read by the web server at request time so a build never bakes it in. Google Consent Mode v2: analytics storage stays denied until the visitor accepts in a short, dismissible prompt; advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always denied; no user id, no profile field, no photo, no insight or scripture text, no location and no query string is ever sent; GA is never loaded on `/admin`, `/dev`, sign-in or account pages. The terms and privacy page names GA and the consent choice, and the choice can be changed from «ملفي».
29. **Dynamic sitemap.** The API serves `GET /sitemap` (sections, pages and last-modified dates) and `GET /sitemap/{section}?page=`; the web app writes `/sitemap.xml` as a sitemap index and `/sitemaps/<section>-<page>.xml` from them. Sections: static public pages, public insights and posts, public place pages of the atlas, and public profiles. Only public, published, non-withdrawn content is listed; a feature that is switched off is not advertised. `robots.txt` points to the index.
30. **Arabic only.** One language, right-to-left; no language switcher and no locale routing. All copy lives in one messages module so a translation stays possible later.
31. **SEO follows `docs/SEO.md`**, the project's SEO playbook (one language, user-generated public pages), including IndexNow pinged last in every production deploy. It refines decision 28: the Google script is injected only after the visitor accepts, so no request reaches Google before consent.
32. **Cookie consent and behaviour analytics.** The owners want full behaviour data — page views, scrolls, clicks, engagement and heatmaps — collected only with consent that meets current rules (GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive, UK GDPR and PECR, Google Consent Mode v2 as required for EEA and UK traffic since March 2024, and the data-protection laws of the Arab countries the audience lives in, such as Saudi Arabia's PDPL).
    - **First visit: a full-screen consent screen** in the product's own design, with «قبول الكل», «رفض الكل» and «تخصيص» given the same weight. Rejecting closes it and the whole product works: no cookie wall, nothing pre-ticked, no dark pattern.
    - **Categories:** necessary (always on, session and security only); analytics (GA4 with enhanced measurement and the product's own events); behaviour and heatmaps. Each is chosen separately.
    - **Proof of consent** is stored on the server: an anonymous consent id, the policy version, each category's choice and the time — no IP address. The choice is asked again after six months or when the policy version changes, and can be changed at any time from the footer and from «ملفي».
    - **Nothing loads before consent:** Consent Mode v2 defaults are all denied; GA and the heatmap tool are injected only after their category is accepted; advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation stay denied.
    - **Never sent to any analytics tool:** profile fields, photos, typed text, insight or scripture text, locations, query strings, email addresses or user ids. Heatmap recordings mask every text input and every user-generated text.
    - **Heatmaps** use Microsoft Clarity (free, Consent Mode aware) configured by `CLARITY_PROJECT_ID`, unless the owners choose another tool; empty means off.
33. **OpenAI is the default AI provider, switchable.** Measured in `docs/BENCHMARK.md`: `gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17` (reasoning off) scored 0.98 with a p95 of 5.7 s against Qwen3.8-27B's 0.97 at 27 s. `AI_PROVIDER=ovh` switches every stage to OVH with no code change; both stay wired and benchmarked.
34. **Contact addresses and support.** `privacy@tabsira.me` is the contact named on the terms and privacy pages; `support@tabsira.me` receives the support form. The support form only emails that address with the visitor's address as Reply-To; there is no helpdesk client in the app. It is rate limited and stores nothing beyond what the mail carries.
35. **Terms of use and privacy policy are accepted at sign-up.** Creating an account (email or Google) requires ticking an unticked box that accepts both, each linked; the accepted versions and the time are recorded as consent rows; a new version asks again at the next sign-in. _Note on enforcement:_ acceptance is recorded only by the e-mail sign-up body and by `POST /auth/legal/accept`; a Google sign-up creates an account with no acceptance and never takes it from a link. The server, not the page, enforces it: every session-authenticated route answers 403 `legal_acceptance_required` (with the current versions) until the account has accepted, except account export and deletion, `/auth/*`, `GET /legal`, `/support` and cookie consent. When Google takes over an unverified password account, the previous holder's acceptance is withdrawn.
36. **Arabic only, ready for more languages.** No locale in the URL (no `/ar`, `/en` prefixes); every visible string, mail and template goes through the messages modules keyed by language, the `lang` and `dir` attributes come from one setting, and layout uses logical CSS properties, so a second language can be added later without moving any URL.

37. **Public ids are Mastodon-style.** Every record whose id reaches a URL or an API response (posts, comments, publications, scans, insights, map entries, notifications, reports) takes a 64-bit primary key from `app.timestamp_id(table)`: the millisecond shifted left 16 bits, ORed with a per-table sequence offset by a salted hash. Ids sort by time and do not reveal row counts. The salt is drawn at random when the function is created and is never in the repository. JSON carries these ids as strings. Accounts keep their internal UUID, which never appears in a public response; people are addressed by handle. Bearer tokens (sessions, consent ids, guest keys) stay random and are never public ids.
38. **The admin area lives at `admin.tabsira.me`, reachable only over the VPN.** No public DNS record: the admins resolve it through the VPN's DNS, nginx listens for it on the VPN interface only, and the certificate comes from a DNS challenge. The API answers `/admin` only for that host (`ADMIN_URL`), sends no CORS headers there, and accepts its state changes only from that origin; `api.tabsira.me/admin` is a 404. Locally it is `admin.tabsira.test`.
39. **Admin two-factor is off by default, in production too.** `ADMIN_REQUIRE_TWO_FACTOR=false` everywhere; an admin can turn the factor on from the panel.
40. **Core first, then phase 2.** This release finishes the core loop end to end, deployable: accounts and legal, photo → honest progress → insight (verse, hadith, «لماذا ظهر هذا؟», small step, chat, «تمّ») → personal world and practice progress, the share card and the public insight page. The social network API merges but its interface waits; the social interface, the atlas («أطلس بصائر العالم») and camera discovery are phase 2.
41. **Reranking uses a small language model, not a CPU cross-encoder.** Measured on 109 gold queries: `gpt-5.4-nano` reranks with MRR 0.770 in about 3 s, while the best cross-encoder (`bge-reranker-v2-m3`) scored 0.667 and took about 20 s per call on CPU, making a scan 38 s instead of the 5–12 s target. The default is the provider's small text model on the top fused candidates, returning ids and scores only; the cross-encoder stays available for a GPU host, and reranking can be switched off.
42. **Tests at the end of each phase.** To go faster, a branch merges during a phase on lint plus the existing tests of what it touches; missing tests and the 100 % coverage gate are done in one test task at the end of the phase, before release. Scripture display and guards, authentication, privacy, and photo or location handling keep their tests and reviews at every merge.
43. **TABSIRA is entirely free.** No payment, no paid plan, no subscription, no premium feature and no advertising, anywhere; every feature is open to every user, signed in or not where the feature allows.
44. **Production requires S3.** Consented photos are kept in a private S3-compatible bucket, and production refuses to start without a usable bucket: at start the API checks it with a 5-second timeout (HeadBucket, then a put and delete of a probe object under `private/` with a random name), and a failure names the bucket and the endpoint host, never a key. A missing or half-filled S3 configuration, or any `S3_*` key set without `S3_BUCKET`, is refused at load. The gunicorn worker exits and the master halts, and the deploy's pre-flight boot fails before any live worker is touched. Development and test default to `STORAGE_BACKEND=auto`: S3 when a bucket is set, otherwise the local disk (`LOCAL_MEDIA_DIR`, `data/media` by default) with a warning at start, never a refusal, so `make dev` works offline. `python -m src.cli.check_config` runs the same probe. The photo rules are unchanged.
45. **The small step's postpone button reads «سأفعله لاحقًا».** First person and a gentle commitment, matching the API's recorded answer; it replaces «أجّل الآن» wherever `tajriba.md` and the design doc name it.
46. **The insight chat answers with `gpt-5.4-mini` by default** on OpenAI (the insight stages' model), at most three answers per insight; the OVH equivalent is the provider's insight-stage model. Switching provider switches it.
47. **Hadith rulings are recorded by the team.** The owners' editors record each dorar.net ruling through the admin rulings queue (task 12.1) or `python -m src.cli.record_ruling`, starting with the rain-scene hadith Bukhari 1032 and 2320; until a hadith has a ruling, its insight shows the verse alone (decision 18).
48. **Embeddings live in their own `vectors` schema with a third Alembic chain.** Vectors are derived, large and rebuildable, unlike the records in `app`: `vectors` holds the scripture embeddings, their run log and any future embeddings (ontology concepts, learning units, insights, places), one row shape per subject (subject key, model, dimensions, document SHA-256, vector). Its chain runs last (`make migrate`: geodata, app, vectors) because its keys point at `app` tables with `ON DELETE CASCADE`; the role's search path adds `vectors`; backups may leave it out because the archive in the owners' bucket restores it (docs/EMBEDDINGS.md).
49. **Local development runs over plain HTTP on port 80, never 443** (amends decision 10's mkcert setup; production stays https only). `http://tabsira.test`, `http://api.tabsira.test` and `http://admin.tabsira.test` are served by the machine's nginx on port 80 with no certificate; `scripts/setup-nginx-local.sh` no longer installs mkcert. Because a browser drops a `Secure` cookie and any `__Secure-` name over plain HTTP, the API sets its cookies without the Secure flag and without that prefix whenever `SITE_URL` is http (`Settings.cookie_secure`, `Settings.cookie_name`); production refuses an http `SITE_URL`, `API_URL` or `ADMIN_URL` at start, so production cookies stay Secure and prefixed. Known limits of plain HTTP at a `.test` name: the service worker (production builds only) and any secure-context API would not run there, and Google's console accepts only https redirect URIs for non-localhost hosts, so Google sign-in cannot be exercised locally. Decided 4 October 2026.
