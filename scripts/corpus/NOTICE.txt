Quran text (tables quran_*)
  Source: Quranpedia.net, «الموسوعة القرآنية», https://quranpedia.net
  Mushaf 2, «مصحف حفص نسخة نصية», from its official dumps, version @QURANPEDIA_VERSION@
  (https://api.quranpedia.net/dumps). Licence: Quranpedia.net Data License, version
  2026-10-01 (https://api.quranpedia.net/dumps/LICENSE.md). The Quran text is the
  shared heritage of the ummah; its digitisation, structuring, verification,
  diacritical correction and metadata are the work of the Quranpedia.net team.
  The text is corrected continuously: keep any copy current through
  https://quranpedia.net/api/v1/changes?since=<version> (TABSIRA applies it daily,
  src.cli.sync_quran). Provided as is, without warranty; verification remains the
  user's duty.
  Derived from it by TABSIRA, never displayed: the folded search copies, and the
  leak guard's skeletons of each verse converted into today's spelling with their
  span view (table quran_verse_standard_guard), which hold no readable text.
